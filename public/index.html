Lineup Card Start your lineup

Give every kid a chance on the field.
No benchwarmers and no angry parents.

Put a player at every position for every inning, in about a second. Then print the card and coach the game.

Why a coach needs this

Youth baseball asks a volunteer to be fair to eleven families at once, before 8am, with a pen.

The rules fight each other

Every player needs infield time early. Nobody should sit twice while another kid never sits. Nobody should stand in right field all game. Holding all of that in your head, inning by inning, is the hard part.

Parents keep count

You may not be counting innings on the bench, but somebody in the bleachers is. A card you can hand over ends the conversation before it starts.

Kids show up late

One player is out, another arrives in the second inning, and the card you wrote at breakfast is wrong. Changing one spot should not mean starting over.

One press, a whole game

Innings run across the top and positions down the side, the way a card should read. Press Build lineup and every spot fills at once, following your league's rules.
